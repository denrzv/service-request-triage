## Limitations

- All 240 messages are synthetic English, written and labelled by one author (an AI assistant under maintainer direction) without a second independent reviewer; real traffic is noisier.
- The taxonomy and policy are illustrative and synthetic; results say nothing about any real bank's routing, legal or SLA obligations, or about non-English performance.
- 75 held-out cases give wide confidence intervals; per-class scores for rare classes are noisy.
- Policy v1 thresholds are uncalibrated no-ops; the JEV_ONLY v1 result is argmax plus categorical rules.
- Threshold candidates come from 165 calibration cases with an illustrative wrong-auto-route target; they are provisional and not applied to policy v1 (FEATURE-007 owns calibration).
- The keyword rules were authored by the same author as the labels, from the development split; they may be optimistic on that split.
- Baselines have no LLM fallback: every ambiguity concern becomes HUMAN_REVIEW.
- jev-latest moves between releases; the resolved model is recorded and replay pins the recorded answers.
- Cost is an estimate from the published per-token price and recorded usage; invoices can differ.
